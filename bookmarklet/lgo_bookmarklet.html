LGO Bookmarklet

Drag the link below to your bookmarks bar, then navigate to https://lotro-wiki.com and click it.

LGO Stats

Workflow: export gear in-game, click the bookmarklet on lotro-wiki.com, paste the plugindata, then click Save TOML... when fetching finishes — the file is saved as lgo_<character>_gearStats.toml. (Falls back to the browser's Downloads folder on non-Chromium browsers.)

Note: the bookmarklet only works when you are already on lotro-wiki.com.
